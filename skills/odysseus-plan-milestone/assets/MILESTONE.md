# M01 — Outcome title

Status: Draft

Sources: link the product and roadmap at their actual output locations (normally `../PRODUCT.md` and `../ROADMAP.md`). Add relevant decisions and existing implementation references.

## Outcome

Describe the observable result or major uncertainty this milestone resolves.

## Requirements and failure behavior

Describe required behavior, including relevant error and recovery cases.

## Acceptance examples and completion evidence

Describe concrete scenarios and observable expected results. Identify useful verification evidence without inventing unavailable commands or claiming tests passed.

## Non-goals and dependencies

State excluded outcomes and actual prerequisites. Separate blocking product decisions from technical investigation.

## Constraints and reasons

State meaningful boundaries and their reasons; link accepted architecture where relevant.

## Suggestions and assumptions

Label optional approaches and unverified assumptions. Use None when not needed.

## Open decisions

Identify unresolved choices, their consequences, and the affected work. Use None when resolved.

## Implementation handoff

Inspect the actual repository before choosing execution steps. Preserve the outcome and acceptance criteria; adapt technical approaches and repair ordinary failures within scope. Bring material product, cost, or consequential-risk changes to the owner. Report results, evidence, limitations, and discoveries in ordinary language. Execution may span multiple steps without creating new roadmap milestones.
